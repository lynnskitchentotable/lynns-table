My story

I learned to cook by being curious.

Cooking became my happy place — part creativity, part meditation, and one of my favourite ways to show love.

I didn't grow up knowing how to cook. I'm not a trained chef, and most of the recipes here didn't begin with perfect measurements. They began with curiosity.

I simply love good food. I love discovering a dish at a restaurant and wondering, Could I make this at home? I love finding an ingredient I've never cooked with, trying a recipe, changing it, tasting it, getting it wrong sometimes, and trying again until it becomes something I genuinely love.

Somewhere along the way, cooking became much more than making dinner. When life is busy and my mind is full, chopping, stirring, smelling and tasting make everything slow down. For a little while, I'm simply there.

My kitchen doesn't have borders. I grew up with Vietnamese flavours at the table, but I also love Italian simplicity, French technique and elegance, Chinese and other Asian flavours, Western comfort food, and the unexpected dishes that happen when those worlds meet.

Restaurant-at-home doesn't have to mean complicated. A few good ingredients, one useful technique and a little care with presentation can turn an ordinary dinner into something special.

But real life isn't always a three-course meal. Sometimes we have twenty minutes. Sometimes we're tired. Sometimes dinner begins with whatever is left in the fridge. Those meals belong here too.

Above everything else, food is love. Cooking for someone can say: I thought about you. I want you to eat well. I want you to enjoy this. And cooking for yourself is an act of care too.

You don't have to be a chef to make incredible food. I didn't know how to cook once either. I learned by starting.

So choose something delicious. Turn on some music. Taste as you go. Make it yours.

Cook with joy. Eat with love. ❤️
